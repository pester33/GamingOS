HOW TO INSTALL GAMING OS oke guys so
1. Get a USB flash drive at least 32 GB
2. Get Rufus or balenaEtcher (thanks TuffTheo for telling me about balenaEtcher )
3. Click my Download button below to get the ISO
4. Make sure your USB is plugged in then open Rufus/balenaEtcher select the ISO click Start and wait
5. Restart your PC and keep spamming F2 or F12 (depends on your device) to open the boot menu
6. Once youre in the boot menu press F6 to put the USB flash drive on top
7. Make sure Secure Boot is OFF and BitLocker is OFF
8. Make sure SATA mode is set to AHCI not RAID
9. Press F10 to save changes and enjoy GamingOS
