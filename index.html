How to Install

Follow these steps carefully to get GamingOS running on your system ok brother?

  1. Get a USB flash drive at least 32 GB.
  2. Get Rufus or balenaEtcher (thanks TuffTheo, ill send discord later guys not now cuz am lazy hehe).
  3. Click the Download button above to get the ISO.
  4. Open Rufus/balenaEtcher, select the ISO, click Start and wait.
  5. Restart your PC and keep spamming F2 or F12 to open the boot menu.
  6. Once you're in the boot menu press F6 to put the USB flash drive on top.
  7. Make sure Secure Boot is OFF and BitLocker is OFF.
  8. Make sure SATA mode is set to AHCI not RAID.
  9. Press F10 to save changes and enjoy GamingOS.
